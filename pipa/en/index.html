Pluck and listen

Allow microphone access to start tuning. Let the app detect the string or select it yourself. The needle points left when the pitch is flat and right when it is sharp.

Tune your way

Choose standard, Drop D, half-step down or custom guitar tunings; standard or custom pipa tunings; and high G, low G or custom ukulele tunings. Adjust the A4 reference frequency and tuning tolerance. Play a reference tone for each string.

Audio stays on your device

Live pitch analysis happens on your device. Microphone audio is not recorded, stored or uploaded. The app includes ads and an optional annual ad-removal subscription.
