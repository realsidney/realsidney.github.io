Privacy Policy

2026-11-02 · Teacho / Teacho · YUTAI WANG

Photos and permissions

No account is required. Photo processing and comparison creation take place on your device. Teacho does not upload photos or modify or delete originals. Comparisons you choose to save are added as new images. iCloud photos are downloaded through the system. Revoke photo, camera and notification permissions in system Settings.

On-device reminders

When daily reminders are enabled, Teacho analyzes a small number of photo thumbnails on your device to recognize scenes such as cats, flowers, and landscapes for memory notifications. When available, the on-device language model refines the wording using scene labels; otherwise, preset wording is used. Photos and scene labels are not uploaded to a server for this feature, and no cloud model is used.

Notification wording and photo identifiers, modification dates, and related information used to reuse wording are cached locally. Cache entries for past dates are cleared. Scene details may appear in Lock Screen notifications. You can manage previews in system notification settings.

Firebase

Teacho uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and Crashlytics to identify and investigate crashes. These services may send app instance identifiers, usage events, app and operating system versions, device information, crash stack traces, and related analytics and diagnostics to Google. Advertising identifier (IDFA) collection is not included. Vendor identifier (IDFV) collection and default advertising personalization signals are disabled. Photos, photo identifiers, scene labels, and generated memory wording are not sent as custom analytics events or crash logs.

Additional analytics and diagnostics details

Firebase Analytics derives approximate location from IP addresses and records app lifecycle events such as launches, screens and sessions. Crash and session diagnostics may include performance and other diagnostic metadata. Analytics and diagnostics may be associated with app instance or installation identifiers. We do not use this information for cross-app advertising tracking.

Local data and support

Reminder preferences and wording caches are stored locally. Turn off reminders, revoke permissions or delete the app to remove local app data. Manage saved photos separately in Photos. When you email support, we process the email address and message you provide to answer your request. Contact realsidney@qq.com for data requests.

Website and third parties

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose provider may process access logs including IP addresses. These pages add no analytics or advertising scripts. See the links below for Google data handling, retention and security information. This policy is updated when features or data practices change.
