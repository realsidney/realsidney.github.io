A little help with your memories.

Get started

Open Teacho and grant full or limited photo access. The app shows still photos taken on the same month and day in previous years. Photos from this year and videos are excluded.

No memories today?

Check that your library contains photos taken on this day in a previous year. With limited access, only selected photos are searched. Manage access in system Settings. Capture dates and the current device time zone affect matching.

Create a comparison

Open Then & Now from a memory photo, take or choose a second photo, adjust framing, and switch layouts. Use the share button to save or share. Camera access is optional.

Missing reminders?

Enable daily reminders in the app and check system notification permissions, Focus and preview settings. Open the app periodically to replenish scheduled notifications; a long period without opening may exhaust the schedule.

Trouble loading iCloud photos?

Check your connection and system iCloud Photos settings, then retry. Reminder scene analysis does not download iCloud originals.

Contact the developer

Email realsidney@qq.com with your device model, OS and app versions, and steps to reproduce the issue. Redact private details in screenshots. You do not need to send personal photos.
